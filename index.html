    FAQ

    Ordering signs online, answered.

    Still unsure which sign is right? Call the shop and we'll help you choose.

    1-800-898-8618
    How does online pricing work?

    Your price updates as you choose material, size, finish, lighting and mounting. When you check out, you pay for the sign itself. We then send a free digital proof, and nothing is fabricated until you approve it.

    Is installation included in the price?

    Installation is quoted separately because access, wall type, height, permits and electrical work vary from site to site. Request an installation quote with a few photos of the wall, or book an on-site survey for larger and lighted signs.

    Can I upload my own logo?

    Yes. Upload your logo at checkout or with a quote request. Vector files (AI, EPS, PDF or SVG) work best, and our design team can help prepare other file types.

    Do you handle sign permits in Los Angeles?

    Yes. Sign permitting is one of our in-house services. We prepare drawings and work with the city when your sign needs approval.

    Can I pick up my order instead of shipping?

    Yes. Choose pickup at our shop on National Blvd, local delivery, or professional installation at checkout.

    What if I need something you don't list?

    Most of our work is custom. Scoreboards, architectural sign packages, restorations and event signage are all quoted by our project team. Start a custom quote and we'll follow up.
